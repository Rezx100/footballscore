# What the first identity was missing

The v1 board (Programme & floodlight / Desk & cobalt / Night ticket + Unit / Weight split / Color split) is **rejected**. This file names the gaps so the next identity does not repeat them. It is not a competitor spec.

Nothing here is locked color or lettering. Palettes for approval: [brand-proposals.md](./brand-proposals.md).

---

## The bar

A category-leading sports brand is ownable at 16px, in a black-and-white fax, and on a packed scores list. The name is custom lettering, not a system font. One chroma is used rarely. Product greys are not the brand color. Data red is not the logo color. There is a construction grid, clearspace, minimum size, and pictured misuse.

footballscore already has meaning (name, mission, principles, voice). It did not have an ownable mark or chroma.

---

## Gaps in the rejected board

| Job | What we shipped | Why it fails the bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark | Geist-like lowercase, one weight or a weight/color split | No custom letterforms, no optical corrections, no ligature or scoreline device. Weight-split and two-color split are startup defaults. |
| Mark | None | A billion-dollar sports identity has a symbol that still reads at favicon size. A soccer ball is banned; so is “no mark at all.” |
| Chroma | Pitch green, cobalt, ember | Green = night pitch. Blue = sports-desk SaaS. Ember = generic LIVE. None of these can be owned. |
| Roles | Signal used as both logo paint and live | Live, red card, follow, and brand accent collided. |
| Construction | None | No grid, clearspace, min size, reverse, or misuse sheet. |
| Type | Scaffolding sans for masthead | Display lettering and product UI type are different jobs. |
| Paper | Warm cream on all three | Territory is right; the *signal* was still category-default. |

## What the category already occupies (do not copy)

Scores products in this category tend to share one of three skins:

1. **Night pitch** — dark olive or black, system sans masthead, green or white LIVE.
2. **Desk utility** — charcoal, one electric blue for active/follow, page title “Scores” instead of a brand.
3. **League broadcast** — heavy italic caps, shield, one official league hue.

Our v1 palettes mapped onto (1), (2), and a quieter (1). The wordmarks mapped onto “clean sans, lowercase, maybe split the last syllable.”

What is *missing* in the category, and therefore available to us:

- An owned metal (not grass, not sky)
- Print authority (programme stock + one dark field color) without looking like a club
- A live/brand chroma that is not green, not blue, not orange
- Custom condensed lettering that could only be `footballscore`
- A 16px symbol derived from score, time, or type — never a ball or a shield-as-trust

## What happens after a palette is picked

1. Three original marks (letter, meaning, abstract) in that palette.
2. Then a custom wordmark / type lockup so `footballscore` is lettering, not Geist Regular.
3. Only after those are chosen: tokens in `brand.md` and CSS.
